Your week with Lobe Limpet

A few minutes of yours, ten minutes a day of theirs.

1

Set it up once

Add your child, pick their packs or snap their revision list, and set exam dates if you have them. It takes a few minutes.

2

They revise a little each day

Short quizzes, flashcards and a daily review that brings back what they're about to forget. Streaks keep it going.

3

Sunday, you see the week

A short email with what went well and what needs a look, so your conversation is "tell me about the Cold War" rather than "have you revised?".
