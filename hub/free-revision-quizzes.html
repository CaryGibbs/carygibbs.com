Why quizzing works

Trying to remember something, then checking, makes it stick far better than reading notes again. Getting some answers wrong is part of how it works: the gap shows what to go back over. Coming back to the same topic a few days later makes it last even longer.

For more on what helps, see our guides to helping your child revise for their GCSEs and KS3 revision tips.
