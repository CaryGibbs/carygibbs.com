Lobe Limpet Plus

Let AI do the heavy lifting when you set up revision.

Lobe Limpet is free to use: students revise, and parents and teachers can write their own notes and questions by hand. Plus adds AI to save hours of setting up.

What you get with Plus

  • Create with AI. Turn your own notes, a document or just a topic name into revision notes and a full set of quiz questions in a few minutes.
  • Upload documents. Build revision from PDFs and Word documents, such as revision guides, specifications and teachers' handouts.
  • Read scans, photos and diagrams. AI reads scanned pages and photos of notes, and describes diagrams, graphs and tables so they can be learnt and quizzed.
  • Suggested resource links. Links to the official exam board pages for each subject.
