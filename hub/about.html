About Lobe Limpet

A revision app that started at the kitchen table.

Lobe Limpet began as a small revision app built by a parent for his own family's GCSEs. The idea was simple: make revision feel less like a mountain and more like a habit, with short quizzes, clear notes and a reason to come back tomorrow.

It worked well enough that friends asked to use it, and it has grown from there into an app for students, parents and teachers.

What we believe about revision

  • Little and often wins. Ten focused minutes a day does more than a night of cramming.
  • Testing yourself is learning. Recalling an answer makes it stick far better than re-reading it.
  • Timing matters. Coming back to a topic just as it starts to fade is what makes knowledge last.
  • Encouragement helps. Streaks, badges and a kind word from home keep motivation up when exams feel far away.

Why "Lobe Limpet"?

Limpets cling to rocks and don't let go, which is exactly what we want your knowledge to do. The "lobe" is a nod to the brain's frontal lobe, the part that does the planning, focusing and remembering that revision needs.

Where we're heading

Lobe Limpet is free to start. Over time we'll add more subjects, a library of shared revision packs, and a Plus plan with extra features. We're a small, independent project, so if you have ideas or spot something that isn't right, we'd love to hear from you.
