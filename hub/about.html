About Lobe Limpet

A revision app that started at the kitchen table.

From the founder

Hi, I'm Cary. I built Lobe Limpet for my daughters. One was getting close to her GCSEs and the younger one was in the early years of secondary school. Their school gave them clear revision lists and good material. What they needed was help making sure they really understood all of it, and that it stuck. As a parent I wanted to support them, but I didn't always know what to ask, or whether something had properly gone in.

So I built something that turns those lists and notes into short quizzes and clear explanations, marks what they actually write, and helps them do a little every day. It also lets me see how they're getting on and which topics need a look, so our conversations about revision are more useful and less nagging.

I've spent my career in technology, so I knew what good software should feel like. When Lobe Limpet became part of our routine at home and friends started asking for it, I opened it up to other families and teachers.

If something isn't working for you, or there's a feature you'd love, please get in touch. I read every message.

Cary Gibbs, founder

What we believe about revision

  • Little and often wins. Ten focused minutes a day does more than a night of cramming.
  • Testing yourself is learning. Recalling an answer makes it stick far better than re-reading it.
  • Timing matters. Coming back to a topic just as it starts to fade is what makes knowledge last.
  • Encouragement helps. Streaks, badges and a kind word from home keep motivation up when exams feel far away.

Why "Lobe Limpet"?

Limpets cling to rocks and don't let go, which is exactly what we want your knowledge to do. The "lobe" stands for the brain's frontal lobe, which helps you plan and focus, and its temporal lobe, which helps you store what you learn: the two parts that do the work of revision.

What makes it different

  • Written answers count. AI marks answers in your own words, so you practise explaining, not just recognising.
  • Flashcards that test you. Every card you make becomes five kinds of quiz question and joins your daily review, pictures included.
  • Progress you can see. Score trends for every subject, weakest topics first, and the questions you keep missing.
  • Rewards that build a habit. XP, levels, 80+ badges and streaks, with a way to win a lost streak back.
  • Everyone together. Students, parents and teachers each get their own view of the same revision.

Where we're heading

Lobe Limpet is free, with no adverts. There's now a community library of revision packs from parents and teachers, checked before they appear, and a Plus plan that uses AI to turn your own notes and documents into revision. Next we're working on building a whole subject from one document, and more ready-made packs. We're a small, independent project, so if you have ideas or spot something that isn't right, we'd love to hear from you.
