Material you build revision from

You can turn material into revision: photos and files (such as worksheets and class notes), text you paste, and links to web pages (such as BBC Bitesize). When you do:

  • Only use material you're allowed to use for your own or your students' revision. School worksheets and public revision websites are usually fine for this; selling or republishing them isn't.
  • The AI reads it to write notes and questions in its own words. For web pages, we keep only the link (shown to students as "Read first"), not a copy of the page. We only read a page when you ask, and we respect sites that don't allow apps to read them.
  • Don't upload personal details about other people, such as names or marks on a worksheet.
  • Packs made from someone else's material are for your own students. Please don't share them in the library unless the material is your own or you have permission.
  • Links to other websites are provided for convenience. We aren't responsible for their content, and their own terms apply.

If you think something in Lobe Limpet uses your work without permission, please contact us and we'll look into it and remove it where appropriate.

You can ask us to remove it at any time, but copies already made may stay with the people who made them. We can turn down or remove packs, for example if they contain mistakes, personal details or material that isn't yours to share.
