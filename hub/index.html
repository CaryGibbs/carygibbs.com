How it works

Does your child struggle to know where to start with revision? This gives them a clear place to begin, from the list in their school bag to revision they'll actually do.

1

Snap the list

Take a photo of the revision list, or upload a worksheet, class notes or a revision guide. Tables, scans and diagrams are fine.

2

We make the revision

Topic notes, flashcards and quiz questions from exactly what school set, ready in minutes. Check and change anything you like.

3

They revise, you see it

Ten-minute quizzes, streaks and badges keep them going. You see time, scores and weakest topics, without nagging.

No list to hand? Pick a ready-made pack for their GCSE or A level subjects instead: sample topics are free, and full packs come with Plus.
