Help & FAQs

Quick answers to the questions we hear most. Can't find yours? Get in touch.

Getting started

What is a revision pack?

A pack is a set of subjects and topics to revise, such as "GCSE Science" or "Year 10 History". Each topic has notes to learn from and questions to test yourself on. A parent or teacher can set packs up for you, or you can choose your own.

Who can create an account?

Students, parents and teachers can all sign up from the sign-in page. If you're under 13, please ask a parent or carer to create your account and link it to theirs.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Lobe Limpet works in any modern browser on phones, tablets and laptops. On a phone, use "Add to Home Screen" from your browser's share menu and it opens like an app.

Revising

What kinds of questions are there?

Quizzes mix multiple choice, true or false, fill in the blank, match the pairs and short written answers. Each quiz is ten questions, so it fits into a spare ten minutes.

How are written answers marked?

Short written answers are marked automatically by an AI model against the key points for that question, and you get feedback on what you included and what you missed. It's very good but not perfect, so treat the mark as a guide and check the model answer.

What's the best way to learn a new topic?

Open the topic's subject map, flashcards or cheat sheet first to get the big picture, then take a quiz. Getting things wrong early is normal and part of how learning sticks.

What is "review"?

Review brings back questions you answered a while ago, timed for just before you'd be likely to forget them. A few minutes of review each day keeps everything you've learned fresh.

How do XP, levels, streaks and badges work?

You earn XP for every quiz, and XP builds your level. Revising on consecutive days grows your streak, and badges reward milestones like trying every question type or finishing a topic.

Parents and teachers

How do I link my child's account?

From your parent dashboard you can add a student or send an invite. Once linked, you can assign revision packs, see their progress and send them a message of encouragement.

How do students join my class?

Each class has a six-letter join code. Students enter it when they sign up, or later from their account, and appear in your class straight away.

Exam boards and free resources

Where can I find past papers and specifications?

Every exam board publishes its specifications, past papers and mark schemes free on its website. Check with your school which board you're taking for each subject, then search the board's site for that subject:

  • AQA
  • Pearson Edexcel
  • OCR
  • WJEC Eduqas (England) and WJEC (Wales)
  • CCEA (Northern Ireland)
  • SQA (Scotland)

Some subjects in your revision packs also have an "Official resources" section with direct links.

When are my exams?

The Joint Council for Qualifications (JCQ) publishes the official exam timetables and the rules for exam day. Your school will give you your personal timetable.

What other free resources are good?

BBC Bitesize has clear explanations, videos and quick tests for most subjects, organised by exam board. Oak National Academy has free video lessons. Both work well alongside Lobe Limpet: learn a topic there, then lock it in here with a quiz.

Your account

I've forgotten my password

On the sign-in page, tap "Forgot your password?" and we'll email you a reset link. If it doesn't arrive, check your junk folder.

How do I delete my account?

Email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it and your revision data. See our privacy notice for more on what we keep.
