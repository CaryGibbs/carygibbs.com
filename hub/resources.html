Free resources we've found useful

Official, free, and the ones we keep coming back to. Links open in a new tab, so Lobe Limpet stays open.

Exam boards

Specifications, past papers and mark schemes, free for every subject. Check with the school which board each subject uses.

  • AQA
  • Pearson Edexcel
  • OCR
  • WJEC Eduqas (England) and WJEC (Wales)
  • CCEA (Northern Ireland)
  • Qualifications Scotland (Scotland, formerly SQA)

Exam dates

The Joint Council for Qualifications publishes the official GCSE and A level timetables. Your child's school will also give them their own personal timetable.

  • JCQ exam timetables

Learning help

For when a topic didn't go in at school: clear explanations, video lessons and practice questions, matched to the curriculum.

  • BBC Bitesize (all subjects, including Scottish National 5 and Higher)
  • Oak National Academy (free lessons up to GCSE)
  • Corbettmaths (maths videos, worksheets and 5-a-day)
  • Physics & Maths Tutor (past questions by topic)
  • Hwb (Wales, with a school login)

If it all feels too much

Some nerves are normal. If they're struggling, the school is a good first step. These are free and confidential.

  • Childline: call 0800 1111
  • Shout: text SHOUT to 85258, any time
  • YoungMinds: advice for parents
  • Urgent help: call 111 and choose option 2
