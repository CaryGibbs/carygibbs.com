Free printable revision planner

Some families prefer paper. Print it, plan the week together, and stick it on the fridge.

Two A4 sheets. The weekly planner gives each day one or two short sessions and a few minutes of review. The topic checklist is for sorting the revision list into "confident", "a bit shaky" and "no idea", which is the best first step we've found. Let your child fill them in, with you as a sounding board: people stick to plans they made themselves.
