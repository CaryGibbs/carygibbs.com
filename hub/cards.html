My flashcards

Make your own sets of cards. Each card becomes quiz questions and goes into your daily review.

How your cards help you revise

  • Flashcards: flip through, then sort into "Know it" and "Still learning" until you know them all.
  • Quiz: every card becomes multiple choice, true or false, type the term, match the pairs and a written answer.
  • Written answers are marked by AI, so you can explain in your own words instead of matching the card word for word.
  • Review today: cards you get wrong come back at just the right time to make them stick.

New flashcard set

Type a term and its meaning on each card. Press Enter to move on (Shift+Enter for a new line).

Tip: copy a set from Quizlet (Export) or a spreadsheet and use Import to bring it all in at once. Add a picture to any card: a diagram, map or labelled photo. With a picture, the definition is optional: a card with just a term and a picture becomes a "what's this?" card, showing the picture first.
