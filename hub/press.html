Short description

Lobe Limpet turns a student's school revision list into notes, flashcards and quizzes, marks their written answers, and shows parents and teachers how they're getting on. Knowledge that sticks.

Longer description

Lobe Limpet is a revision app built by a parent, with help from his daughters and the friends' families who tested it along the way. Snap or upload the revision list a school has set, and Lobe Limpet turns it into clear topic notes, flashcards and quizzes, with written answers marked by AI and spaced review that brings topics back before they're forgotten. Parents and teachers see progress and the topics that need a look, so conversations about revision are more useful and less nagging. It works alongside class notes from Key Stage 3 to A level, and for anything else someone needs to remember. Lobe Limpet is free to start.

Using our brand

Please do

  • Write the name as Lobe Limpet, two words with capitals. The logo itself is lower case.
  • Keep some clear space around the logo, about the height of Lobey's eyes.
  • Use the white logo on dark backgrounds and photos.
  • Link to lobelimpet.com where you can.

Please don't

  • Stretch, squash, rotate or recolour the logo or Lobey.
  • Add shadows, outlines or other effects.
  • Redraw Lobey, or make Lobey say things we wouldn't.
  • Use our brand in a way that suggests we endorse something we don't.
